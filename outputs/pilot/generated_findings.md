# Pilot findings

All price results are **non-executable price-move proxy** measures, not profit, return, or realizable PnL. Remaining move is non-executable and not profit. Missing values are not treated as zero. Timestamps are second-formatted; milliseconds are not identifiable.

Updated-trade coverage among clean eligible pairs: 5s: 3.3% (26/789 clean eligible); 10s: 4.6% (36/787 clean eligible); 30s: 11.2% (86/769 clean eligible); 60s: 14.6% (111/760 clean eligible); 120s: 19.9% (119/597 clean eligible); 300s: 22.6% (67/297 clean eligible); 600s: 37.8% (68/180 clean eligible); 1800s: 100.0% (105/105 clean eligible). Across the delay profile, sparse update coverage and high delayed-state age make seconds/minutes price-opportunity decay weakly identified/under-resolved.
